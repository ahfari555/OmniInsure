Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At OmniInsure, accessible from our website, protecting your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we operate.

Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Collection)

All auto, home, life, and health insurance estimations are processed locally within your web browser. Your inputs and personal parameters are never uploaded or stored on any remote server.

Google AdSense & Cookies

We use third-party vendors, including Google, to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing history.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
